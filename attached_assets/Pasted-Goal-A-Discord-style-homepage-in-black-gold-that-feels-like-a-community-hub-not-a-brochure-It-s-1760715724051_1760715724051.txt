Goal: A Discord-style homepage in black & gold that feels like a community hub, not a brochure. It should look like Discord’s 3-column layout on desktop, with clean, compact spacing on mobile.

⸻

1) Overall Look & Feel
	•	Brand: Black background, gold accents, white text. High contrast, tight spacing.
	•	Type: Headings in a strong, readable serif or display font; body in a clean sans. (Think: bold headline, calm body.)
	•	Copy to use:
	•	Main title: LEGENDS OF THE PARK
	•	Tagline (small, under title): DEFEND WHAT’S LOCAL
	•	Subheadline (one sentence): Where winning turns into real change.
	•	Primary CTA: Choose Your Park
	•	Secondary CTA: Learn How It Works

⸻

2) Discord-Style Layout (Desktop)

Match the Discord 3-panel layout:

Left Rail (Navigation / Parks)
	•	Fixed left column ~260–280px.
	•	Top: LOTP logo.
	•	Below: Navigation items (“Home”, “Parks”, “Schedule”, “Membership”, “Sponsors”).
	•	Section: Parks (Season One) as a vertical list with small circular thumbnails (use your park images or placeholders).
	•	Bottom: “Help / FAQ” + “Contact”.

Center Column (Main Feed)
	•	Width: fluid; it’s the main stage.
	•	Hero block at the top (uses one of your gold-on-black hero images). Overlay the title + tagline + subheadline + CTA button.
	•	Below the hero, stack short, card-style sections:
	1.	“Choose Your Park” card with a clear button.
	2.	“Latest Schedule” card (teaser copy + “View schedule”).
	3.	“Become a Member” card (one-line pitch + “See membership”).
	4.	Sponsors strip (gold boxes for logos).
	•	Cards should be compact (no giant top/bottom padding) and separated by subtle dividers, like Discord messages.

Right Rail (Community / CTA)
	•	Fixed right column ~300–320px.
	•	“Become a Legend” sticky panel: short blurb + “Choose Your Park” button.
	•	Bubble wall preview: show circular user/profile bubbles “clinging” around the park emblem (think floating cluster). Tap/hover reveals name + park.
	•	Optional: “Online now” style micro-list (coach, captains, etc.).

⸻

3) Mobile Layout (Compact, No Excess Space)
	•	Convert the 3 columns into stacked sections:
	1.	Top App Bar with the logo on the left and a hamburger icon on the right (opens the left rail as a slide-in drawer).
	2.	Hero (image + title + tagline + subheadline + CTA) with tight vertical rhythm:
	•	Title top margin: ~16px
	•	Spacing between title → tagline → subheadline: ~6–8px each
	•	CTA top margin: ~12–16px
	3.	Cards (Choose Your Park, Schedule, Membership, Sponsors) stacked with 16px gaps between them.
	4.	Right-rail content becomes a section near the bottom: “Become a Legend” + compact bubble wall.
	•	Bottom Navigation (optional but recommended):
	•	Fixed 4 tabs: Home · Parks · Schedule · Membership
	•	Keeps the page feeling “app-like,” similar to Discord mobile.

⸻

4) Spacing & Sizing Rules (the main problem you reported)

Use small, consistent spacing. The current site feels “bulky” because margins/padding are too large and inconsistent.
	•	Core space tokens: 4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 24px. Avoid anything bigger than 24px on mobile except the hero outer margin (max 32px).
	•	Heading sizes (mobile):
	•	H1: ~28–32px
	•	H2: ~22–24px
	•	Body: 16px, line-height ~1.4–1.5
	•	Buttons (mobile): Height ~44–48px; border radius subtle (6–8px). One line of text; no giant paddings.
	•	Cards (mobile): Internal padding ~16px. Card gap between sections: 16px.
	•	Section max-widths: Center column shouldn’t stretch endlessly. Constrain content to ~1040px on desktop; on mobile it’s full-width with 16px side gutters.

Acceptance check for “no more big gaps”:
	•	No vertical gap larger than 24px in any stacked mobile section (except the hero).
	•	Headline → tagline → subheadline → button never have more than 8px between each line group.
	•	Cards have exactly 16px top/bottom padding, not more.

6) Interaction Notes
	•	Choose Your Park button opens a modal or page with a scrollable list of parks (search + filter).
	•	When a user selects a park, show a Park Page with:
	•	Park hero image/emblem
	•	“Defenders bubble wall” (circular avatars clinging around the park emblem). Avatars are small, tight, and clustered—no overlap on top of the emblem; they “orbit” it.
	•	Join/Follow park button.
	•	Keep animations subtle (fade/slide 150–200ms). Nothing bouncy.

⸻

7) Accessibility & Performance
	•	Color contrast must meet AA on black backgrounds.
	•	Ensure tap targets are at least 44x44px on mobile.
	•	Compress images; serve responsive sizes. Avoid loading giant desktop assets on mobile.

⸻

8) QA Checklist (what “done” looks like)
	•	Desktop shows the three-panel Discord layout with fixed left/right rails.
	•	Mobile shows stacked sections with tight spacing and no oversized gaps.
	•	Hero text uses the exact copy above; CTAs are visible without scrolling too much.
	•	Bubble wall appears on park pages and as a small preview on the homepage right rail (desktop) / a section near the bottom (mobile).
	•	Navigation works: Home, Parks, Schedule, Membership, Sponsors.
	•	All sections feel compact, not airy; nothing looks “too big” or “too far apart.”
